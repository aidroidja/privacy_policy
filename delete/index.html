Data Deletion — Followers Unfollowers Tracker

Package: followers.unfollowers.insta.tracker.aidroid

This app is developed by AidroidJa. Most app data is stored only on your device.

How to delete your data

  1. Open Followers Unfollowers Tracker.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Clear All Data. This deletes followers, followings, posts, and insights stored on the device.
  4. Tap Logout to remove the Instagram session stored on the device.
  5. Uninstall the app to remove remaining local data.

We do not keep a server copy of your Instagram followers list. Third-party services such as Google AdMob, Google Analytics, and Firebase Crashlytics may keep their own data under their policies.

To request deletion, email aidroidja@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data” and the app name Followers Unfollowers Tracker.
